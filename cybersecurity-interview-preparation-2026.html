FIVE MOCK ROUNDS

REHEARSE
THE PRESSURE.

Record each round. Review clarity, assumptions, prioritisation, technical accuracy, and whether you connected risk to the business.

25 MIN

FOUNDATIONS

Networking, operating systems, cryptography, IAM. Ten rapid questions; explain one answer on a whiteboard.

40 MIN

BLUE TEAM

Triage a suspicious sign-in, map evidence to ATT&CK, draft a containment plan, then write a short stakeholder update.

45 MIN

SECURITY DESIGN

Threat-model a public API. Define trust boundaries, abuse cases, controls, telemetry, residual risk, and trade-offs.

40 MIN

CLOUD + APPSEC

Review a flawed cloud architecture and CI/CD flow. Prioritise identity, secrets, supply chain, network, and detection controls.

50 MIN

FULL LOOP

Five technical questions, one incident scenario, one architecture problem, and two behavioural questions.

10 MIN

YOUR CLOSE

Deliver your 90-second introduction, two STAR stories, and five high-signal questions for the interviewer.
